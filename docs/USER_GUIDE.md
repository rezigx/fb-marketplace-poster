# User Guide — FB Marketplace Multi-State Poster

## What this app does
Post one product to Facebook Marketplace across **all 58 Algerian wilayas** at once. Buyers from every region message you directly on **your WhatsApp number**.

---

## Quick start

### 1. Activate your license
1. Open the app.
2. Go to the **Settings** tab.
3. Paste your **license key** (sent to you after payment).
4. Set your **WhatsApp number** — this is printed on every listing so buyers message *you*.

### 2. Log in to Facebook (once)
- The app opens Facebook and asks you to log in.
- Log in with your own account. Your session is saved — you won't need to log in again.

### 3. Build a product
1. Go to **Products**.
2. Fill in **Title**, **Description**, **Price (DA)**, **Category**.
3. Click **+ Add images** to attach product photos.
4. Click **Save product**.

### 4. Post to all wilayas
1. Go to the **Dashboard**.
2. Click **▶ Post to All Wilayas**.
3. Watch the progress bar + log. The app posts one listing per wilaya, with human-like delays to stay safe.

---

## Tips for best results (and staying safe)
- **Use your real, established Facebook account** — new/empty accounts get flagged faster.
- **Don't over-post**: keep to the daily cap (default 30). The delays are there to protect you.
- **Good photos + clear price** get more replies.
- **Check your WhatsApp** — all buyers message you there directly.

## Renewing your subscription
- When your month ends, the app says your license is expired/inactive.
- Pay via **CCP / BaridiMob**, then message the seller with your name. Your license gets re-activated and you can post again immediately.
